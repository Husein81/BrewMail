# Available Coffee Origins

## Current Selection

BrewMail currently ships single-origin beans from three regions: Ethiopia (Yirgacheffe and Sidamo), Guatemala (Antigua and Huehuetenango), and Colombia (Huila and Nariño).

## Blends

We also offer a signature House Blend combining Colombian and Brazilian beans. The blend profile emphasizes chocolate, caramel, and a smooth finish.

## What We Do Not Carry

Our selection does not include beans from Southeast Asia (including Vietnam and Indonesia), Hawaii, or any African regions outside of Ethiopia. We also do not sell tea, cocoa, or other non-coffee beverages.

## Rotation Schedule

Origins rotate seasonally based on harvest cycles. The current selection is available at brewmail.com/origins and updates monthly.
