# Subscription Pause Policy

## Overview

BrewMail understands that life happens. If you need to take a break from your coffee deliveries without fully cancelling your subscription, you can pause it at any time through your account dashboard.

## How Long You Can Pause

You can pause your BrewMail subscription for up to 90 consecutive days per calendar year. The 90-day limit resets each January 1st. If you reach the 90-day cap mid-pause, your subscription will automatically resume on day 91 with your next scheduled shipment.

## How to Pause

1. Log in to your BrewMail account at brewmail.com/account.
2. Navigate to Subscription Settings.
3. Click the "Pause Subscription" button.
4. Choose a resume date (optional) or leave open-ended.
5. Confirm your selection.

Pausing takes effect immediately. Any shipment already in processing or transit will still be delivered.

## How to Resume

You can resume at any time from the same Subscription Settings page. Click "Resume Subscription" and your next shipment will be scheduled based on your original delivery cadence (1st and 15th of each month).

## What Pausing Does Not Do

Pausing is distinct from cancelling. Pausing retains your account, your preferences, your delivery address, and your payment method. Cancelling terminates all of these and requires a new subscription to restart.

Pausing does not refund any already-shipped orders. For refunds, see the Refund Policy document.
