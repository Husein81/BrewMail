# Cancellation Policy

## How to Cancel

To cancel your BrewMail subscription, log in to your account and go to Subscription Settings. Click "Cancel Subscription" and confirm.

## When Cancellation Takes Effect

Cancellation takes effect immediately. You will not be billed again, but you will still receive any shipments that have already been dispatched or are in processing.

## Cancellation Is Permanent

Cancellation terminates your account's subscription record. To restart your deliveries after cancelling, you must create a new subscription — your previous preferences and payment information will not be restored.

## Cancel vs. Pause

If you want a temporary break rather than ending the subscription, see the Subscription Pause Policy. Pausing retains your account and preferences; cancellation does not.
