# Refund Policy

## Overview

BrewMail stands behind the quality of every shipment. If something isn't right, we want to make it right.

## 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

We offer a 14-day satisfaction guarantee from the date your order is delivered. If your coffee arrives damaged, is incorrect, or you are not satisfied for any reason, email support@brewmail.com within 14 days of delivery to request a full refund.

## How Refunds Are Processed

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5 business days of approval. Credit card refunds may take an additional 2-5 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

## What Is Not Covered

We do not offer refunds for subscriptions that were not paused or cancelled before the next billing cycle. If a shipment has already been dispatched, you are responsible for that shipment's cost. To avoid future billing, see the Cancellation Policy or Subscription Pause Policy.
