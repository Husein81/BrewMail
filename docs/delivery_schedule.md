# Delivery Schedule

## Shipment Dates

BrewMail subscriptions ship on the 1st and 15th of each month. Your assigned shipment date is set when you subscribe and does not change unless you request it in Subscription Settings.

## Delivery Times

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the continental United States, 5-7 business days to Canada, and 7-14 business days internationally.

## Carriers

Domestic shipments are sent via USPS Priority Mail. International shipments are handled by DHL. Tracking numbers are emailed to the account holder at the moment the carrier picks up the package.

## Delays and Lost Shipments

If your shipment is more than 7 business days past its expected delivery date, contact support@brewmail.com with your order number and we will file a trace with the carrier.
